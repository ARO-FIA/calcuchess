# CalcuChess

CalcuChess is a free chess platform that brings together tools for analyzing games, playing against bots, solving puzzles, and improving your chess skills.

Whether you're looking to review a recent game, practise against a computer opponent, or sharpen your tactical abilities, CalcuChess aims to provide a simple and accessible way to enjoy chess and learn more about the game.

## Features

### Game Analysis

CalcuChess allows you to analyze your games from Chess.com and Lichess, making it easier to review your performance and learn from your decisions.

By going back through your games, you can identify mistakes, explore missed opportunities, and better understand important positions. Reviewing your games is one of the most useful ways to improve at chess, and CalcuChess aims to make that process easier.

### Play Against Bots

Play chess against computer opponents to practise your skills and test different strategies. Whether you want to get more experience, try out new ideas, or simply play a game, bots provide a convenient way to practise at your own pace.

### Chess Puzzles

Challenge yourself with chess puzzles designed to help you practise tactics and calculation. Work through different positions, look for the strongest moves, and develop your ability to recognise tactical opportunities during games.

### More Features

CalcuChess is designed to bring useful chess tools together in one place. As the website continues to develop, the goal is to expand its features and make it an increasingly useful resource for players of different skill levels.

## About the Project

CalcuChess was created to make useful chess tools more accessible to players. The project began with the idea that players should have more opportunities to analyze their games and practise their skills without needing to pay for additional features.

The focus is on building a practical chess platform that combines useful tools in one place while continuing to improve the experience for its users.
